Questions Owners Actually Ask

Straight answers, before you spend a dollar.

How much does the assessment cost, and what do I get?

Two tiers. The $500 Snapshot: a readiness score across 7 dimensions, a scan of your digital presence, your top 3 AI plays, and a 90-day plan — in about 3 business days. The Strategic Assessment ($5,000–$10,000): a 55–65 page boardroom-grade report adding a full competitor audit, where your market is headed, and a complete financial model — in 2–3 weeks. Both credit forward if you continue with us.

We tried AI already and it fizzled. Why would this go differently?

Because roughly 95% of AI pilots fail — and almost always for the same reason: they automated the wrong thing. Nobody found the constraint first. That's exactly what the assessment does: it finds the two or three narrow wedges where AI provably pays in your operation, and skips everything else. The Snapshot will tell you why your last attempt stalled.

What's a Fractional AI Officer, and why would I need one?

The AI platforms ship major changes monthly — features get absorbed, prices shift, better paths open. Big companies hire a Chief AI Officer to manage that. The fractional seat gives you the same coverage from $1,500/month: a monthly report proving what your AI returned, your AI policy and governance kept current, vendor changes handled, spend reviewed, and a quarterly re-assessment. The industry's churn becomes our problem instead of yours.

Will this replace my people?

No — and we put that in writing. We never automate a judgment call: pricing, diagnosis, approvals, anything a customer would call "your judgment" stays human. The machine only checks objective rules — is the packet complete, did the lead get answered, do the two systems match. Your people get their hours back for the work that actually needs them.

Can I just pay for an hour of advice first?

Yes. The AI Strategy Session is $300 for 75 minutes on your agenda — what to buy, what to skip, whether a vendor pitch is real — with a written recap after. It's paid so you get the honest version, not a sales call, and it credits in full toward a Snapshot or Assessment within 30 days. There's also a $500/month advisory retainer if you want a standing sounding board.

Which AI platform do you work with — and what about the "memory" they all have now?

All of them: Claude, ChatGPT, Gemini, Copilot. We're platform-agnostic on purpose — the plan fits the stack you already pay for. And built-in memory is real now, but it's vendor-locked and invisible. Your business knowledge — customers, pricing, voice, process — goes into governed files you own, portable across platforms, auditable, and correctable. That's the difference between an assistant with impressions and an operation with a manual.
