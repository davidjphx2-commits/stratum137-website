INSIDE BOTH TIERS 02

The wedges — small programs your team could start next week.

Most of your core work needs human judgment, and we'll never tell you to automate it. What the assessment finds instead are the wedges: high-frequency friction points with objective pass/fail rules — the packet that bounces for a missing form, the lead that waits days, the data typed twice. Each wedge comes quantified from your own numbers: what it costs you now, what the fix returns, why it's near-zero risk.
