The Question Everyone Asks

"It has memory now. Doesn't it know my business?"

No — and the difference is where most of the value hides. Built-in memory keeps impressions about you. Your business needs a file of record.

Built-in memory

What the platform remembers on its own

  • Automatic notes the AI decides to keep — you don't choose
  • Invisible until you dig into settings; capped in size
  • Locked to one vendor and one login
  • Can vanish — memory-loss incidents are documented
  • Knows your habits; doesn't know your pricing, clients, or process

A Company Brain

What we build — business knowledge you own

  • Deliberately written: customers, jobs, pricing rules, your voice
  • Plain files you can open, read, and correct in one place
  • Works on Claude, ChatGPT, or Gemini — switch anytime, keep everything
  • Backed up like any file — it can't silently vanish
  • New employee's AI knows the business on day one

Memory is a nice-to-have. The Company Brain is infrastructure. It's built into every engagement we run →

What AI actually does well today.

Honest maturity ratings for the jobs a small business cares about — from our own daily use, not vendor slides. The bars are our field judgment: how reliably it delivers with a proper setup.

Reading & triaging email

Ready now

Sorting what matters from noise, flagging money and stuck deals. Boringly reliable.

Drafting in your voice

Ready now

Replies, proposals, letters — excellent once your voice and rules are written in. You still hit send.

Summarizing meetings & documents

Ready now

Voice memo in, filed summary and action list out. One of the highest-value habits to build.

Spreadsheet analysis

Ready now

Finding the broken formula, explaining the numbers, showing its math. Verify money figures — it shows the work so you can.

Scheduled multi-step automations

Ready with setup

Morning briefings, follow-up tracking, weekly reviews — solid when the connections and guardrails are configured right.

Answering your phones

Ready with setup

AI receptionists book real jobs 24/7. Needs proper training on your business and a human escape hatch.

Acting alone across your tools

Supervise it

Agents that browse, click, and buy on your behalf are improving fast — keep approval checkpoints on anything that spends or sends.

Being right about facts, unsupervised

Supervise it

Still confidently wrong sometimes. The fix is grounding it in your documents — another job for the Company Brain.

ChatGPT makes AI a household word. Impressive, forgetful, disconnected — a brilliant intern with amnesia, locked in a browser tab.
