Case Study 01

Federal Opportunity Platform — every contract worth chasing, scored by AI, on one screen.

The client's team was hunting government opportunities by hand — SAM.gov searches, state portals, spreadsheets, tribal knowledge. Winnable contracts slipped past unseen, and nobody could say what the whole pipeline looked like.

We built them a private web platform that ingests federal and state/local opportunities automatically, then scores every one against the client's actual service lines with AI — a 1-to-10 fit score with the reasoning shown. An executive dashboard rolls it up: a 50-state heat map, deadline radar, top buying agencies, and a per-rep pipeline that exports straight to Salesforce.

The executive view — live counts, team pipeline, opportunity heat map across all 50 states, and the next ten deadlines.

Every opportunity carries an AI fit score — and shows its reasoning: keyword matches, service-type fit, contract value, agency priority.

Click any state for the full picture: who to sell into, which federal agencies are buying there, and scored state & county bids.

Case Study 02

Municipal Bid Tracker — fourteen city portals, one morning glance.

Public-sector bids are posted across a maze of city and county procurement portals — each with its own login, its own format, its own deadlines. Miss a posting window and the work goes to whoever was watching.

This tracker turns that maze into one screen: every tracked municipality, every open bid, deadlines ranked by urgency, incumbent intelligence on upcoming re-bids, and one-click launch into each portal. The team opens it with their coffee and knows exactly where to aim the day.

Shown with sample data. Open-bid counts, portal quick-launch, and per-city bid tables with status, fit, and the recommended next action.
