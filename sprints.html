The Wedge Doctrine

We never automate a judgment call.

Most of your core work needs experienced human judgment — and you're right not to risk it. The win nobody shows you is the narrow wedge: high-frequency friction with objective pass/fail rules. That's where AI is boringly, provably reliable.

Stays human — always

  • Pricing decisions and exceptions
  • Diagnosis, medical or mechanical
  • Claims, credit, and approval calls
  • Anything a customer would call "your judgment"

The wedge — objective rules, high frequency

  • Is the packet complete before a human reviews it?
  • Did the lead get a response inside the hour?
  • Does system A match system B, or did re-keying drift?
  • Is every required doc in the file before the deadline?
Sprint Menu

The wedges that pay — each with its number.

Every sprint starts from your assessment roadmap (or a scoping call), and every one ships with a KPI we agree on before you pay a dollar. Boring is beautiful: these don't demo like magic — they just hit their number every day.

Document Completeness Check

Every packet, application, or work order verified complete — forms, signatures, photos — before it reaches your reviewer. Kills the days-long missing-document bounce.

KPI example: first-pass completeness 61% → 95%

Intake & Lead Triage

Every inbound lead qualified against your rules, routed to the right person, logged in the CRM — with a response going back inside the hour, in your voice.

KPI example: median response 26 hrs → under 1 hr

Re-Keying Eliminator

Data typed into one system flows into the other — quotes to invoices, field notes to job records — checked, matched, and flagged when the numbers drift.

KPI example: 9 hrs/wk of double entry → 0

Follow-Up Sequences

Estimates, invoices, and open loops chased on schedule until they close — every touch staged for your approval or fully automatic, your call per sequence.

KPI example: estimate follow-up rate 40% → 100%

Onboarding Packets

New client or new hire: the right documents generated, sent, tracked, and chased — a 30-day paperwork trail compressed to a same-day packet.

KPI example: onboarding admin 6 hrs → 40 min
